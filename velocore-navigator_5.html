Connect your Google Maps API key

This app uses Google's free, no-credit-card "Demo Key" for maps, address search, and routing. The key is tied to your Google account and stays saved only in this browser — it's never sent anywhere else.

  1. Click "Get a key" below (opens Google's page in a new tab)
  2. Sign in with your Google Account and accept the terms
  3. Copy the key it generates and paste it here

Optional — Map ID for the behind-the-vehicle chase view (tilted, rotates to face your direction of travel). Without one, the map stays flat and just follows you top-down. Free to create in Google Cloud Console; if it doesn't work with a Demo Key project, the app falls back to the flat follow view automatically.

Optional — Default map location. Where the map opens before you connect the bike or search a route. Leave this blank to use your browser's location automatically (you'll get a one-time permission prompt); set it here to skip that prompt and always open in the same place instead.

Waiting for a device…
Requires Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android) for the bike connection — Web Bluetooth isn't supported in Safari or Firefox. Distance travelled comes from your bike's own reported speed when available, or is otherwise estimated from your power output using a simple outdoor-cycling physics model — a reasonable approximation, not a precise measurement. Routes are recomputed fresh each time (including on resume) rather than stored long-term, per Google's terms of service — only your start/destination and progress are saved locally in this browser so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent anywhere except Google's mapping APIs; your bike data never leaves this tab.

Your Rides

Check the box on 2 or more Rides to progress them together — swipe or use the arrows on the Navigator tab to switch between them while they all keep counting your distance.

Create a Ride

Click anywhere on the map to add a waypoint. Drag a waypoint to fine-tune it, or click it again to remove it.
